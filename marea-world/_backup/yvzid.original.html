The Artist
YVZID
"Urban Poet" — Rabat, Morocco

Elyazid Bensada, born August 21, 1997. A singer-songwriter whose sound fuses rap, urban pop, and jazz into something distinctly his own. Every track carries the weight of Moroccan identity filtered through a modern, global lens.

Since his 2022 debut EP Eurêka, followed by Méditerranée and the album ASL (2023), he has built a catalog that moves between vulnerability and defiance. Collaborators include Figoshin, S13, Raid, Inkonnu, and VALEN.

Featured in BarzFact's Top 10 Moroccan Rap Songs of 2025. MAREA WORLD is the next chapter.
